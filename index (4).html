Our story

Why we built this

Touchline started as a school project at the American International School of Cape Town — but the idea behind it is bigger than that. A lot of kids grow up loving football without ever having a real ball, a decent pair of boots, or someone to show them the basics. Most people don't think about that gap until it's pointed out to them.

We wanted to build something that does two things at once: teach the game properly, for free, to anyone with a phone or a laptop — and turn that into a way of funding real gear for kids and schools that are going without.
